# Project Charter / Statement of Work (SOW)
## GullyGigs — Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services

**Document Owner:** Project Sponsor / Co-op Founding Team
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft for Approval
**Date:** August 2026

---

## 1. Project Purpose

To design, build, and launch a digital platform that connects verified household and community service providers with local clients, structured as a worker-owned cooperative — offering lower fees, higher worker earnings, community-based trust verification, and democratic governance over platform policy.

## 2. Business Case / Justification

- Existing gig platforms extract 20–35% commission and offer workers no equity, no voice, and unstable algorithmic income.
- Informal/unorganized service markets lack verification, dispute resolution, and pricing transparency.
- A cooperative model captures an underserved segment: workers and clients who want fairness, transparency, and community accountability — a strong differentiator with civic bodies, RWAs (Resident Welfare Associations), and NGOs as go-to-market partners.

## 3. Project Objectives

| Objective | Success Measure |
|---|---|
| Launch MVP in one pilot city | Live app with 3 service categories within 6 months |
| Onboard verified service providers | 200 verified co-op members by end of Phase 1 |
| Demonstrate lower-cost value prop | Commission held at ≤12% throughout pilot |
| Prove community trust model | ≥80% of new providers verified via community vouching, not ID checks alone |
| Establish governance foundation | Working member voting mechanism live by Phase 3 |

## 4. Scope

### In Scope
- Booking and scheduling platform (web + mobile)
- Multi-layer verification system (ID, background check, community vouching)
- Payments, escrow, and payouts
- Worker equity/dividend tracking dashboard
- Ratings, disputes, and community reinvestment fund tracker
- Governance/voting module (Phase 3)

### Out of Scope (Phase 1)
- International expansion
- Non-household service categories (e.g., B2B enterprise services)
- Full blockchain-based equity ledger (may be evaluated later)
- In-house background-check infrastructure (third-party provider used instead)

## 5. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role/Interest |
|---|---|
| Founding Co-op Board | Overall direction, fundraising, governance design |
| Service Provider Members | Primary platform users; income and equity stakeholders |
| Household/Community Clients | Demand-side users |
| Community Verifiers (RWAs, local leaders) | Trust network partners |
| Engineering Team | Product build and delivery |
| Legal/Compliance Advisor | Cooperative registration, labor law compliance, payments compliance |
| Investors/Grant Bodies (if any) | Funding, expect responsible use of capital, may hold non-voting stakes only |

## 8. Assumptions

- Pilot city has sufficient smartphone/internet penetration among target workers.
- A third-party verification/background-check provider is available and compliant with local regulation.
- Cooperative legal structure can be registered within the project timeline.

## 9. Constraints

- Regulatory: cooperative societies and gig-worker classification laws vary by state/country and must be researched before Phase 1 completion.
- Budget: bootstrapped/grant-funded — no venture capital that would compromise the ownership model.
- Timeline: MVP must launch within 6 months to validate the model before scaling budget commitments.
