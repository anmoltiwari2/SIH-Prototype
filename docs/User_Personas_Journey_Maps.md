# User Personas & Journey Maps
## GullyGigs Platform

---

## Persona 1: Meena — The Service Provider

- **Age:** 34 · **Location:** Urban pilot city, lives in a rented flat with family
- **Occupation:** Currently does informal house-cleaning and cooking work via word-of-mouth and one gig app
- **Tech comfort:** Owns a basic Android smartphone, comfortable with WhatsApp and simple apps
- **Goals:** Stable, predictable income; fair treatment; some say in how the platform she depends on is run
- **Frustrations:** Existing gig app takes ~25% commission, deactivates her account without explanation if ratings dip, no way to appeal
- **Quote:** *"I do the work every day — why do I have no say in how the app treats me?"*

### Journey Map — Meena (Provider Onboarding to First Payout)

| Stage | Awareness | Sign-Up | Verification | First Job | Payout | Equity Realization |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Action** | Hears about GullyGigs from local RWA/community group | Downloads app, registers via phone OTP | Uploads ID, gets community-vouched by 2 known neighbors | Accepts first booking via app | Job completed, payment auto-released after 24hr hold | Sees patronage points accrue on dashboard |
| **Thoughts** | "Is this legit? Lower fees sound too good." | "Simple enough, similar to other apps." | "I like that my neighbor vouching counts for something." | "Client seems more at ease seeing my verification badge." | "Finally, I see exactly what was deducted and why." | "Wait — I actually own a piece of this?" |
| **Emotion** | Skeptical | Neutral | Reassured | Confident | Trusting | Invested/Motivated |
| **Opportunity** | Clear USP messaging in onboarding | Reduce sign-up friction | Fast-track known/vouched workers | Highlight trust badges prominently to clients | Transparent fee breakdown by default | Make equity dashboard visually simple, not finance-jargon-heavy |

## Persona 2: Rohan — The Household Client

- **Age:** 29 · **Location:** Apartment complex in pilot city
- **Occupation:** Software professional, works long hours, limited time to vet household help
- **Tech comfort:** High — uses several apps daily
- **Goals:** Reliable, safe, reasonably priced help without the anxiety of "who is this stranger in my home"
- **Frustrations:** Past bad experiences with unverified help found via classifieds; existing gig apps feel like a lottery on quality
- **Quote:** *"I don't just want a 4-star rating — I want to know someone in my community actually trusts this person."*

### Journey Map — Rohan (Discovery to Repeat Booking)

| Stage | Discovery | First Booking | Service Experience | Post-Service | Repeat Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Action** | Sees GullyGigs mentioned in RWA WhatsApp group | Browses cooking-help category, filters by verification level | Provider arrives, service completed | Rates provider, sees fee breakdown | Books same provider again via saved favorites |
| **Thoughts** | "A co-op model? Interesting, and cheaper." | "I like seeing community vouches, not just stars." | "Smooth, professional." | "Good to see so little goes to the platform vs the worker." | "This is now my default for household help." |
| **Emotion** | Curious | Reassured | Satisfied | Impressed | Loyal |
| **Opportunity** | Community-channel marketing (RWA partnerships) | Make verification badges visually prominent in search results | Ensure smooth in-app communication with provider | Show client the community fund impact of their booking | Favorites/rebooking flow to build retention |

## Persona 3: Sunita — The Community Verifier

- **Age:** 45 · **Location:** Secretary of a Resident Welfare Association (RWA)
- **Role:** Trusted local figure, knows many household workers personally
- **Goals:** Help her community access safe, reliable services; wants recognition for her local trust network
- **Frustrations:** No formal way to vouch for people she already trusts; existing platforms ignore local social capital entirely
- **Quote:** *"I already know who's reliable in this neighborhood — I just never had a way to put that to use."*

### Journey Map — Sunita (Recruitment to Active Verifier)

| Stage | Recruitment | Onboarding as Verifier | First Vouch | Ongoing Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Action** | Approached by GullyGigs community team for RWA partnership | Registers as a Community Verifier, brief training on process | Vouches for a known local worker during sign-up | Tracks her vouching history and its impact via dashboard |
| **Thoughts** | "This formalizes something I already do informally." | "Straightforward, not bureaucratic." | "I feel responsible, but it's a good kind of responsibility." | "I can see the difference I'm making in my community." |
| **Emotion** | Interested | Confident | Careful/Responsible | Proud |
| **Opportunity** | Partner directly with RWAs/community bodies for verifier recruitment | Keep verifier training lightweight | Cap/weight vouches to prevent misuse (see RAID R3) | Recognize top verifiers publicly (non-monetary incentive) |
