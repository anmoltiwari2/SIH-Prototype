# RACI Matrix — GullyGigs Platform

**R** = Responsible (does the work) · **A** = Accountable (owns the outcome, signs off) · **C** = Consulted (input sought) · **I** = Informed (kept updated)

| Activity / Deliverable | Co-op Board | Product Lead | Engineering Lead | Design Lead | Legal/Compliance | Community Verifiers | Marketing Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Project Charter & SOW | A | R | C | I | C | I | I |
| PRD & Feature Prioritization | C | A/R | C | C | I | I | C |
| Technical Architecture (TDD) | I | C | A/R | C | I | I | I |
| UI/UX Design & Wireframes | I | C | C | A/R | I | I | C |
| Verification Policy Design | A | C | C | I | R | R | I |
| Commission Rate Structure | A | R | C | I | R | I | C |
| Payment Gateway Integration | I | C | A/R | I | C | I | I |
| Community Vouching Process | C | R | C | C | C | A/R | I |
| Legal/Co-op Registration | A | C | I | I | R | I | I |
| Governance/Voting Module | A | R | R | C | R | I | I |
| Dividend Calculation Logic | A | R | R | I | R | I | I |
| Marketing & Onboarding Campaign | C | C | I | C | I | C | A/R |
| QA & Release Sign-off | I | A | R | C | I | I | I |
| Dispute Resolution Policy | A | R | I | I | R | R | I |
| Community Reinvestment Fund Allocation | A/R | C | I | I | C | C | I |

## Notes

- Where **A** and **R** are held by the same role, that role both executes and is accountable for the outcome — common for focused technical deliverables.
- The **Co-op Board** holds final accountability (A) on anything with financial, legal, or governance impact, consistent with the cooperative's member-first structure.
- **Community Verifiers** are Responsible for the vouching mechanism itself even though they aren't a formal internal team — this reflects the platform's reliance on external trust networks.
- This matrix should be revisited at the start of each project phase, as roles may shift (e.g., Marketing Lead becomes more central in Phase 2–3).
